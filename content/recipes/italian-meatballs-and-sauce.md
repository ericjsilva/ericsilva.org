A slow-simmered red sauce with sausage and meatballs. Serve it over linguine or rigatoni with a big dollop of ricotta or fresh grated/shredded parmesan cheese.

## Ingredients

### Sauce

- 1 lb sausage (all sweet, all hot, or a mix)
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 cup good red wine
- 2 large cans (28 oz) whole tomatoes, San Marzano if you can (whirred in a blender or food processor)
- 2 small cans (6 oz) tomato paste
- Water (fill each empty paste can once ~12 oz.)
- 1/2 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp fresh cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp dried basil
- 1 tbsp dried parsley
- 1 tsp sugar (optional, to cut the acid)

### Meatballs

- 1.5 lbs meatloaf mix (or 1 lb lean ground beef and 1 lb ground pork)
- 1 cup breadcrumbs
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan
- 1 egg
- 2-3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 tbsp kosher salt
- 1 tsp fresh cracked black pepper
- 1 tbsp dried basil
- 1 tbsp dried parsley

### To serve

- 1 lb Rigatoni or linguine pasta
- Ricotta or parmesan cheese
- Fresh hoagie rolls (if making meatball sandwiches)

## Directions

1. In a large sauce or stock pot, brown the sausage. It should be browned all over but does not need to be cooked through. Remove it from the pot.
1. Drain all but 2 tbsp of the grease. If not grease is left, add a tbsp of olive oil. Saute the garlic in the grease over medium-high heat for 1-2 minutes, until brown. Watch it closely, garlic burns quickly.
1. Add the red wine to deglaze the pot. Reduce for 4-5 minutes.
1. Add the whirred tomatoes and the tomato paste. Fill each empty paste can with water and add that to the pot.
1. Add a good pinch of salt, some cracked pepper, basil, and parsley. Stir until mixed well.
1. Lower the heat to low and return the sausage to the pot along with any juices that collected.
1. Make the meatballs: combine the meatloaf mix, breadcrumbs, parmesan cheese, garlic, egg, salt, pepper, basil, and parsley. Mix with your hands just until incorporated, no more. Roll into balls and bake them at 350° for 20 minutes.
1. Add the meatballs and drippings to the sauce.
1. Cook on low for a few hours, until the sauce is a deep red.
1. Serve over pasta with ricotta or parmesan cheese.

## Notes

- If anyone in the house gets reflux from acidic foods, add a teaspoon of sugar to the sauce while it cooks to cut the acid.
- The meatballs are optional. The sauce is good with just the sausage.
